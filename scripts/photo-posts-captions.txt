THE PHOTO POSTS (October 2026): INSTAGRAM AND FACEBOOK

Pictures from the 3 October 2026 shoot, two photographers, two Lightroom albums (scripts/photo-posts.json says which picture goes where and how it is cropped;
scripts/instagram-photo-posts.mjs makes the slides). Each post is an Instagram carousel and the same pictures as one Facebook post:
  assets/social/photo-posts/<post>-01.jpg … <post>-NN.jpg   1080x1350 (4:5); the first slide is the cover and the last is the close, the ones between are the photographs alone.
Everything that matters is in the middle 1080x1080 square of each slide, with a blurred copy of the photograph above and below it, because Instagram squared the first post (it cut
135px off the top and bottom of every picture) and a square crop must lose nothing. (assets/social/shoot/ holds the same pictures under the old names.)
Alt text, one line per slide, is under each post below, in slide order. Instagram's limit is 20 pictures a post.

Both platforms, always: a post is made for Instagram and for Facebook together (the owner's rule from October 15, 2026).
No street address or town on the pictures or in the captions (the hashtags carry the area), and one emoji in each.
No "link in bio" anywhere: the bio link was set to the donation page for the drive (as of October 11), so the posts say labyrinth.vision.
Every post credits both photographers by their Instagram handles, @rayharkness and @002.Deleon, in the last line before the hashtags: in the Instagram caption they are @mentions (no photo tags and no collaborator invites), and in the Facebook text they are plain text, which says "on Instagram".
The Instagram line has no full stop after the handle, because a full stop straight after a handle can be read as part of it.
Children are in these pictures: they are for the academy's own marketing, under the photo release in the waiver.
Alt text names nobody and uses no pronouns for the people in a picture: who is in it is not something these captions say.
