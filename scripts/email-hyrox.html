Hi Labyrinth fam!

We’re putting together a group of kids interested in training for HYROX Youngstars Houston, April 3–4, 2027!

HYROX is a worldwide fitness race that combines running with functional fitness stations. HYROX Youngstars is the kids’ version, with age-appropriate running distances, movements, and weights. Stations include SkiErg, sled push/pull, rowing, carries, lunges, and more.

Training
The plan

For now, kids should continue with their regular BJJ training. We’ll also start introducing some HYROX movements during our Strength & Conditioning classes.

Once tickets drop in November, we’ll begin more structured training. Kids planning to race are encouraged to do:

  • At least one Strength & Conditioning class per week
  • One HYROX-specific workout per week, combining running with HYROX movements and stations

We’re planning to hold the HYROX-specific workouts at a gym with SkiErgs, rowing machines, and other equipment we’ll need, hopefully very close to our gym! 🤞

This will give the kids several months to gradually build strength, endurance, and familiarity with the race format.

If your child is interested, add them to the list on our page, or reach out to Professor Shaun. Knowing the number and ages of interested kids will help us plan the training schedule.
