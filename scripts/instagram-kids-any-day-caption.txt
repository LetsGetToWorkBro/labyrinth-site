==================== CAPTION ====================
Kids can try a free class any day of the week 🥋

Every day has a class a new student can start in, so pick the day that works for your family. No experience needed.

Ages 3 to 15, in age-matched groups (youth wrestling goes up to 17). Swipe for every class by day and age.

Our advanced No-Gi classes are on there too. They need a gray-white belt or 2 years of wrestling, so new students start in the other classes.

Wear a t-shirt and shorts, and we'll lend you the gi. The first class is free, with no commitment.

We're enrolling kids now. If your child has been curious about jiu-jitsu, this is the easiest way to find out.

Book at labyrinth.vision (link in bio), or call or text (281) 393-7983.
