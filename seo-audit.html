Where Labyrinth stands in Google search

The standard Careful Machine audit for labyrinth.vision: every page crawled, live Google results read from two vantage points, search demand measured, and the academy's standing graded area by area against its Fulshear competitors and its own October 2026 baseline.

B-

Overall grade. The strongest site in this week's audit round by a wide margin. The fight is narrow: two academies in the same town, a review count gap, and one parent phrase where Labyrinth does not appear.

Where things stand

Labyrinth has a verified Google profile at 5.0 stars, the broadest keyword footprint of any academy in Fulshear, the first organic result for its full name phrase, and a site with no structural defects. A parent searching "brazilian jiu jitsu fulshear" finds Labyrinth at the top of the page.

The contest is with two competitors in the same town. Liberatus Jiu Jitsu, five minutes away, holds 192 Google reviews to Labyrinth's 65, and the two academies trade the top map pack seat depending on where the search is read from. Gracie Barra Fulshear holds 114 reviews and the weight of a global brand. And on the phrase that brings in the most new families, "kids martial arts fulshear," Labyrinth does not appear in the map pack at all, in any read, six weeks after the October baseline measured the same gap.

What we checked

Every page on the site (all 49 addresses in the sitemap), the robots file and the AI-readable llms.txt, live Google results read in a browser, twelve standardized Google reads through DataForSEO (a national vantage and a Fulshear vantage), measured search volume and difficulty for the gym's money phrases, the domain's full ranked keyword footprint, backlink counts for Labyrinth and the two Fulshear competitors, the Google profile state through today's pack reads and directory profiles, and the October 2026 Local Falcon grid scans as the baseline.

Technical foundation: A-

The crawl is clean. All 49 sitemap addresses return 200, a made-up address returns a real 404, www redirects to the apex in one hop, and every page carries its own title, description, and a canonical that points at itself. The homepage runs about 4,700 words with location and FAQ schema, correct name, address, phone, and coordinates, and six tap-to-call links. Program pages carry Service and FAQ schema. robots.txt names the AI crawlers on purpose and points at the sitemap and llms.txt, which is live.

What holds it off an A: the pricing page's Product schema still has no image and no aggregateRating, the open item behind Google's Merchant listings flag from November 15. The homepage schema's review count says 55 while the profile holds 65. The site's schema hours and the Google profile's hours disagree. At least six newer blog posts have no tap-to-call link, and most posts carry one image with no alt text. calendar.labyrinth.vision sits in the sitemap with about 60 words and no meta description.

First fix: add image and aggregateRating to the pricing page schema and refresh the homepage review count to 65.

Search visibility: B

The BJJ phrases are won or shared. "bjj fulshear" puts Labyrinth first in the map pack from the national vantage and second from the Fulshear vantage. "brazilian jiu jitsu fulshear" puts it second in the pack from both vantages, with the homepage as the first organic result in the national read. "kids bjj fulshear" puts it first in the Fulshear pack. The measured footprint backs this up: 216 ranked keywords in the US index, about 362 estimated visits a month, 68 keywords in the top ten. That is the broadest footprint in Fulshear. Gracie Barra's domain ranks for 81 keywords, Liberatus's for 21.

The gaps are specific. "kids martial arts fulshear": absent from the pack in both vantages, and absent from the first twenty organic results in the standardized read (the browser read of the longer wording "kids martial arts classes Fulshear TX" does put labyrinth.vision first organically; both reads are reported, and the pack absence is consistent everywhere). "self defense classes fulshear": absent from pack and organic results. "martial arts near me" from Fulshear: absent from the pack. "wrestling classes fulshear": absent, despite a wrestling program page; the pack seats go to Be Like Water, Fulshear Fight Club, and Richmond Wrestling Academy. "bjj katy tx": absent entirely; Katy's pack belongs to Revolution Dojo (506 reviews) and the Katy schools. The index also shows heavy churn, 184 of 216 ranked keywords newly recorded and 187 lost in the same window, so the footprint is broad but not settled.

First fix: aim the kids program at the parent phrase (fix 2 below).

Local presence: B+

The Google Business Profile is verified, active, and correct on name, address, phone, and website, at 5.0 stars with 65 reviews. Weekly Google posts and photos run on Mondays. Bing was completed in October. Directory presence is broad: BJJ Gyms, the jiu jitsu gym directories, Chamber of Commerce, Wellhub, and the Team Legacy listing that Labyrinth owns after the merger.

Two things hold the grade. The first is review volume. Labyrinth's 65 reviews trail Liberatus (192) and Gracie Barra Fulshear (114), and review count is the visible difference in the pack reads where Labyrinth sits second. The second is consistency: the hours conflict between the site schema and the profile hours (the site says Sunday opens 10:30, the profile hours say 11:00, and weekday closing differs by an hour), the Chamber listing still showing 45 reviews, Apple Maps still blocked on its business ID, and the unfinished tail from the October sweep (Facebook About, BBB, Jits.gg, Nextdoor, Foursquare).

First fix: settle one true set of hours and publish it in the schema, on the profile, and on the site, the same day.

Content: B+

The site says what the gym sells, in the words buyers search. Six program pages at 1,250 to 1,630 words each with Service and FAQ schema. Eighteen blog posts, each aimed at a real question: what BJJ costs in Fulshear, starting in your 30s, 40s and 50s, homeschool and after-school options, the neighborhoods around the academy, BJJ against wrestling for kids. The cost post targets a phrase family with measured demand and zero difficulty ("how much does bjj cost," 260 searches a month, KD 0).

What is missing is aim at the two gaps. Nothing on the site is built to win "kids martial arts fulshear" as parents type it; the kids page wins the BJJ wording and stops there. There is no self-defense page or section with real weight behind it, though self-defense searches measure 33,100 a month nationally at KD 0 and the gym teaches it inside the BJJ programs. The newer posts also carry the tap-to-call and alt text gaps noted in Technical.

First fix: rebuild the kids page's opening and headings around the parent phrase without giving up the BJJ wording it already wins.

Authority: C+

Measured: 213 backlinks from 94 referring domains, domain rank 155 of 1,000. That sits level with Gracie Barra Fulshear's domain (rank 159, 221 backlinks, 89 domains) and ahead of Liberatus (rank 80, 114 backlinks, 79 domains). Real links exist: industry directories (BJJ Academy Finder with 12 backlinks, Martial Arts Local with 12), the merged academy's old domain, Wellhub, Cornerman, a press release pickup.

The quality is the gap. The long tail of referring domains is rank-0 spam of the same kind found on every site audited this week. There are no weighty local links: no school or community organization links of consequence, no local press. Rank 155 with this mix means the number is respectable and fragile at the same time.

First fix: earn three real local links this quarter (community organizations, event coverage, sponsors) before any cleanup of the spam tail.

The next 60 to 90 days, if the fixes run in order

The review habit matters more than anything else in this report. Ten or more new reviews a month closes half the gap to Gracie Barra by February and puts real pressure on the pack seats Labyrinth holds second. The kids phrase work (profile services, page aim, reviews that mention kids classes) makes a pack seat on "kids martial arts fulshear" contestable inside the quarter; that phrase is the front door for new families. The realistic path is B+ overall by February, with the BJJ pack leadership defended rather than shared.

  2. Win a seat in the "kids martial arts fulshear" pack. Baseline: out of the pack today, average rank 7.00 in October.
  3. Settle the hours. One true set, published in the site schema, on the Google profile, and anywhere else hours appear. The schema and the profile currently give different Sunday and weekday answers.
  4. Finish the pricing schema and refresh the rating data. Add image and aggregateRating to the pricing page's Product schema (open since November 15), and update the homepage schema's review count from 55 to 65.
  5. Give the money posts their phone number back. Add tap-to-call to the six newer blog posts that lack it, and alt text to post images in the same pass.

Then, in order: 6. build a real self-defense answer on the site; 7. finish the listings tail (Apple Maps in a sitting, the Yelp public page verified, the Chamber review count corrected, then Facebook About, BBB, Jits.gg, Nextdoor, Foursquare as the sweep left them); 8. decide the Katy question on purpose, aim at the Katy edge of Fulshear or record Fulshear-only as the decision; 9. earn three real local links this quarter; 10. give calendar.labyrinth.vision a meta description or take it out of the sitemap; 11. close out the stream.labyrinth.vision asset check left open from November 15.
