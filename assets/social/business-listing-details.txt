==================== LONG DESCRIPTION ====================
Labyrinth BJJ is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu academy in Fulshear, Texas, teaching adults and kids from age 3. Adults train Gi and No-Gi in morning, midday and evening classes, with open mat on Sundays. Kids train in three age groups (3 to 6, 7 to 12 and teens), and youth wrestling is for ages 7 to 17. We also run a competition team, MMA conditioning and strength and conditioning classes. Our instructors include black belts and brown belts, and nobody is ever made to compete. Beginners are welcome and your first class is free. Memberships are month-to-month with no long-term contract, and sauna and cold plunge access is available as an add-on. Serving Fulshear, Katy, Cinco Ranch, Richmond and the west Houston area.
