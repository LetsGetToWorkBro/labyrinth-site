What We Teach in Wharton

Kids kickboxing and jiu-jitsu, women's self defense jiu-jitsu, adult kickboxing and adult jiu-jitsu at Labyrinth BJJ Wharton. Every one starts with a free first class.

If it is for a child, it is kids kickboxing and jiu-jitsu. Kids classes are on the timetable. Tell us your child's age on the form and we will confirm the right class. Other ages are not on the schedule yet, so tell us your child's age on the form. If it is for you and you want self defense, it is women's self defense. If you want jiu-jitsu classes for adults, it is adult jiu-jitsu, and it does not matter that you have never done a combat sport, because most people who start have not.

Class times and prices are on the schedule and pricing pages. Adult class times have not been announced yet.
