The Wharton gym

A NEW LABYRINTH GYM FOR WHARTON

Labyrinth BJJ Wharton is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu gym in Wharton, Texas, part of the Labyrinth family of gyms. It is led by Joe Herrera, a brown belt under Prof. Anthony Curry, who founded Labyrinth in 2021.

Brazilian jiu-jitsu is a grappling art built on leverage, position and control. There is no striking in it. It is a practical way to get fit, to learn real self-defense, and to be part of a room full of people who are working on the same hard thing.

We are still setting the Wharton schedule. Until it is ready, the best thing you can do is put your name on the list below: you will hear first when class times, prices and the address are confirmed, and your first class is free.

Lineage

A lineage you can check

Joe trains under Prof. Anthony Curry, a black belt under Matt Leighton of Citadel BJJ. In jiu-jitsu a belt is awarded by a person, so who stands behind the rank matters.

Beginners

Built for people who are new

You do not need to be in shape, flexible or athletic. Most people who start jiu-jitsu have never done a combat sport, and that is who a first class is for.

Your pace

Competition is your choice

Labyrinth has a strong competition record, and competing is always optional. Plenty of students train for fitness, self-defense and community and never enter a tournament.
