Most people searching for a gym do not search for a city. They search for whatever is near them, their neighborhood, their side of the highway, the route they already drive. So this page is the practical version: where we are, and what that means depending on where you live.

By neighborhood

Every figure below is road miles and minutes with no traffic, measured to our door, so they are the best case. Put your own street into a map app at the time you would drive and trust that over us.

Cross Creek Ranch

About five miles and eleven minutes: out of Cross Creek Ranch on FM 1093, then Canyon Fields Drive and Reese Lake Drive to Fulshear-Gaston Road. We used to be inside it and now we are a drive away, but if you are choosing between us and somewhere in Katy, we are still the nearer of our two academies: about five miles against about nine to the Katy academy.

Cross Creek West

The same drive from the western side: about five miles, eleven minutes. Cross Creek West is one of the fastest-growing parts of Fulshear, and the Katy academy is about eleven miles from it.

Churchill Farms

North of FM 1093 and off FM 1463: about five and a half miles, thirteen minutes. Churchill Farms is a smaller, established community of around five hundred and seventy homes, and it is almost level between our two academies, because Katy is about six miles away.

Tamarron

North-west, off FM 1463: about seven miles, fourteen minutes. Tamarron is enormous and still building (over four thousand homes planned) and zoned to Lamar Consolidated ISD with schools inside the community. It is the place where our two academies are almost the same drive, with Katy about six miles away, so the choice is about the room rather than the road.

Jordan Ranch

Down the Texas Heritage Parkway and then along FM 1093: about eleven miles, twenty-one minutes. Jordan Ranch has grown fast (its own elementary school is open), and it is the one Fulshear community that is nearer to our Katy academy, about eight miles away. Go and look at both.

Fulshear Lakes

About nine miles and eighteen minutes, along FM 1093. The Katy academy is about fifteen miles from Fulshear Lakes, so for Fulshear Lakes this is the nearer of our two by some distance.

The middle of Fulshear and the older neighborhoods

The middle of town is about six and a half miles and thirteen minutes away. We have not measured every street (Fulbrook, for one), so put yours into a map app to be sure.

Richmond, Rosenberg and the south

FM 723 is the road. Rosenberg is about ten miles and seventeen minutes, and the old center of Richmond is about fourteen miles and twenty-three minutes. Harvest Green and Aliana are about eleven miles by the Grand Parkway and FM 1093.

Katy, Cinco Ranch and further east

We have a Katy academy too, with Coach Jared Vevera as head coach, and from Cinco Ranch and most of Katy it is the nearer one: about six miles from Cinco Ranch, against about seven and a half to Hidden Lake Ln. If you are east of the Grand Parkway, ask us which location actually suits your week better. We would rather you trained consistently at the closer one than heroically at the further one for six weeks and then stopped.

Timing it around school

The thing that decides whether an activity survives the school year is not enthusiasm. It is whether the drive works on a Tuesday in November.

Our kids classes run in the late afternoon and early evening, and our adult schedule runs from early morning through to evening, including 6:30 am classes several days a week, which exist precisely because some parents can only train before the house wakes up.

The full timetable is on the schedule. What is worth doing before you commit anywhere is the boring exercise: pick the class you would actually attend, and drive the route at that time of day, once. It answers the question better than any map.

A note on choosing by distance

Distance matters more than people expect and less than they fear.

It matters because consistency is the entire game in jiu jitsu. Two classes a week for two years beats four classes a week for three months, and the gym you can reach easily is the gym you will still be attending next spring.

It matters less than you fear because fifteen minutes is not far. If a gym twenty minutes away is clearly the right room for your family (the right coaches, the right feel, the right way of speaking to children) take the twenty minutes. Do not choose a place you do not like because it saved you eight minutes.

Go and look at two or three. Ours is free to try, as are most.
